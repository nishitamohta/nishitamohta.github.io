In her own words

Why I Wrote This Book

I didn't set out to become a writer. I trained as an architect, worked as a designer for 4 years, and then spent years in rooms with creative founders, helping them build businesses that actually fit their lives. At every stage, I saw the same quiet struggle show up: talented people feeling stuck and being far too hard on themselves about it.

‘Inspired’ is what I wish someone had handed me on my hardest days. It isn't a manual. It's a friend that'll walk with you to overcome creative blocks, one chapter at a time.

— Nishita

Publishing it exactly the way I wanted meant doing it myself. Read: Why I Chose to Self-Publish →

The voice of the Inner Critic feels deafening in our own minds. But judgement-free conversations with fellow creatives are like flashlights in the dark. They remind us: we're not alone.

That reminder has an almost-medicinal quality to it, and ‘Inspired’ is a collection of exactly those conversations.
It helps you find your way back to creating.
