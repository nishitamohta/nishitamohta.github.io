I partner with founders to build a business that fits their life, so that they can stop being the most overburdened employee in it.

Welcome to my virtual HQ

I believe:

Running a business is a
cognitive and emotional sport.

And founders need support just the way elite athletes do. Athletes rigorously train for both technique & mindset. They don't just practice on the field, but they train the mental game alongside it, because performance depends on both.

Founders are expected to perform at peak excellence. But conventional business advice means that their systems are built on borrowed blueprints. And the founder's mental health and quality of life is at best a side effect.

I work with founders on both:

01

Cognitive Strengthening

As an ICF-trained Leadership Coach, I work as your thinking partner to build resilience, strengthen decision making and achieve deeper clarity on the business goals that are unique to you.

02

Frameworks

As a business consultant with an expertise in creative studios, I work with you to build systems and structures for how you actually think and for the team you aspire to have.

Neither one works alone.

A stronger mind with no system still burns out.

A better system run by a depleted mind still breaks down.

Both, together, is the whole discipline.
